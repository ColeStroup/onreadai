# Audit Plain-Language Standard v1

## Audience

Write for a business owner who may never have edited a website. Default explanations should be understandable around grade 5-7, while preserving technical evidence behind disclosure controls.

## Required Shape

Every validated issue explains:

1. What this means.
2. Why it matters without promising results.
3. The first action to take.
4. Whether the owner can reasonably do it.
5. Which specialist can help.
6. How Onread will check the work.

Use short sentences, define necessary terms immediately, and prefer a specific action over a broad checklist. Avoid unexplained acronyms, fear language, guarantees, and vague instructions such as "optimize SEO" or "improve engagement."

## Classifications

- Technical issue: an objective material condition supported by complete evidence.
- Meaningful opportunity: evidence supports a practical improvement, often after semantic review.
- Optional refinement: a preference that does not imply failure or reduce the validated score.
- Strength: verified positive evidence.
- Coverage note or limitation: what Onread did or could not verify.

The Consultant defaults to simple language and may provide Standard or Technical detail when requested. It must label uncertainty, ground advice in the saved audit, and never imply that a suggestion is a measured fact.
